Update and Release Live Session Recordings

Overview

After each live virtual instructor-led session, CLEAR makes available a recording of the session for attendees who either were unable to attend the session, or those who would like to review the session.

For the following programs, new recordings must be made and shared for every cohort:

  • Advanced Concepts in Regulatory Governance (ACRG)

  • Executive Leadership Program for Regulators (ELPR)

  • Getting the Most From Your Board – Skills for Effective Chairing (BCT)

  • Skills for the Regulatory Administrator – Staffing a Board (SAB)

These programs encourage attendees to share challenges and experiences that they have faced, which they may not wish for those outside of their cohort to hear. To foster an environment that encourages this sharing without the fear of reprisal, recordings of the sessions are ONLY shared with the current cohort and are removed once the cohort is complete.

All other programs (mainly any NCIT program) share a pre-recorded already available session, as this privacy concern is not an issue for these audiences.

Update the Recordings after the ILT (when required)

Step Action
1 Download the recording from the CLEARlearning@clearhq.org zoom account.
2 Upload the video to CLEAR’s Vimeo account.
3 Edit out the portion of the recording before the instructor begins, breaks, and the portion after the cohort is dismissed
4 Add closed captioning to the recording
5 Add the thumbnail (course logo file)
6 Set the end screen to “Thumbnail”

Once the video is edited, copy the embed link into a text editor. The copied content will look like this:

Step Action
1 Navigate to the course in TalentLMS in the Instructor role.
2 Hover over the recorded version of the session then click the edit option that displays in the popup menu.
3 Click in the Web address (URL) field and paste the highlighted content above in the field.
4 Click the Save and view button to confirm the video loaded properly.

Note: You must use the embed link from Vimeo for this process to work properly. The standard share link does not contain all the information necessary to make this process work.

Activate the Recorded Session Module

Once the video link is updated (if required) or you are ready to share the recorded content follow the process below.

Step Action
1 From the course homepage, locate and hover over the recorded session module.
2 Click the Activate menu option.

Notify Cohort of Video’s Availability

Step Action
1

In TalentLMS, locate the CLEARLearning@clearhq.org account from the users list, and login as that user.

Note: By logging in as the CLEAR Learning account:

  • Replies will go to the clear learning account instead of your personal account

  • You will have access to all cohorts assigned to CLEAR Learning (which should be all cohorts)
2 Click on the Messages drop-down menu at the top of the page and select the Send Message option.
3 Click in the Subject field and enter Recording of Today’s Session is Now Available
4

Click in the Body field and enter:

A (pre-recorded/recorded) version of today’s session is now available in the CLEAR Learning portal for your review. If you were unable to attend today’s live session, you must watch the recording to receive credit for attending this portion of the program.

If you have any questions or need additional assistance, please contact clearlearning@clearhq.org or reply to this email.

Thank you,

CLEAR Staff
5 Click the Send message button.

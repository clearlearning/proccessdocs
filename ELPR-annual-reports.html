Overview

As part of the Executive Leadership Program for Regulators (ELPR), participants have the option to upload an annual report from their organization. These reports are used by the program's subject matter experts (SMEs) and instructors to better understand participants' regulatory contexts and tailor the program accordingly. The instructors will also use one or two of the reports as teaching tools during the program.

This process covers how to locate and download submitted annual reports from TalentLMS and get them to the appropriate instructors before the program begins.

When to run this process

Download and distribute annual reports after the pre-work deadline has passed and before the first live session. This gives instructors time to review the materials before the program begins.

Applies To

  • Executive Leadership Program for Regulators — In-Person Training

  • Executive Leadership Program for Regulators — Virtual Training

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Log in and switch to the Instructor role

Log in to TalentLMS. Switch to the Instructor role using the role switcher at the top of the screen if you are not already in that role.
2

Navigate to the course

From the Instructor dashboard, locate and open the appropriate ELPR course — either the in-person or virtual version, depending on the cohort you are working with.
3

Find the annual report module

On the course homepage, locate the first section. Inside it you will find the Sample Annual Report module.
4

Open the submissions list

Hover over the name of the Sample Annual Report module. A small menu will appear. Click the Submissions link in that menu.
5

Sort by submission date

You will not be able to filter the submissions list, but you can sort it. Click on the Submission Date column header to sort by date. Sort so that the most recent submissions appear first.
6

Identify the correct cohort's submissions

Because ELPR programs do not run frequently, you will be able to see a clear gap in the submission dates that marks the boundary between cohorts. Stop downloading when you reach submissions from a previous cohort.
7

Download each file

For each submission in the current cohort, hover over the participant's username. In the Options column, click the Download Answer icon. A PDF will open in a new browser window. Save the file to a location of your choosing on your computer. Repeat for each submission.
8

Send the files to instructors

Once all files have been downloaded, send them to the program's instructors and SMEs using the appropriate method based on the number of files — see the table below.

Sending Files to Instructors

Use the following guidance to choose the right delivery method based on how many reports were submitted.

Situation Recommended approach
1–2 files Attach the files directly to an email and send to all instructors and SMEs for the program.
3 or more files Upload the files to WeTransfer (wetransfer.com) and include the download link in your email to instructors. WeTransfer links are active for 7 days by default — let instructors know when the link will expire.

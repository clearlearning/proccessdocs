Overview

This guide covers the process for setting up and managing sessions in the Zoom dashboard before a session, and for using Zoom host tools while facilitating a live virtual session. Log into Zoom at zoom.com using the CLEAR Learning account, available in OnePassword.

Note: Sessions created through TalentLMS (instructor-led modules) are automatically created in Zoom. The process for creating those sessions is covered in the Create Live Sessions in TalentLMS process guide. This guide covers Welcome Sessions (created manually) and everything you do inside a live session as the host.

Part 1: Zoom Dashboard Setup

Complete all setup in the Zoom dashboard before the session begins.

Quick Process Guide

Creating a Welcome Session

Step Action
1 Log into Zoom using the CLEAR Learning account (credentials in OnePassword). Go to the Meetings menu.
2 Click Schedule a Meeting in the top right corner.
3 Enter the meeting name (e.g., NCIT Basic Welcome Session — April 2026).
4 Set the start date, time, and duration using the When fields. Welcome sessions are typically 1 hour.
5 Confirm the time zone is set to Eastern Time.
6 Check the Registration — Required checkbox. This generates the registration link used in the Novi event listing and TalentLMS welcome email.
7 Leave all other settings at their defaults. Click Save.

Module evaluations are no longer attached to Zoom sessions. Every module, instructor-led or asynchronous, has its own evaluation unit in TalentLMS. See Updating Module Evaluation Links for the current process.

Adding Polls to a Session

Most polls have already been created in Zoom. You select which ones apply to each session.

Step Action
1 Open the session in the Zoom dashboard. Go to the Polls/Quizzes tab.
2 Click Filter for Meeting to scope the list to this session.
3 Deselect all polls that do not apply, or click Deselect All and then check only the polls you need.
4 Click Save.

Creating a Poll on the Fly: While in the meeting, If a new poll is needed, click the + button in the top right corner of the Polls/Quizzes tab. Enter the title and questions, configure settings, and click Save. Run it during the session the same way as any other poll.

Starting a Session

From the Meetings menu, hover over the session title and click the Start button that appears. Log in 15 minutes before the scheduled start time.

Part 2: Live Facilitation — Host Tools

Everything in this section applies once you are inside an active Zoom session.

Quick Process Guide

Step Action
1 Waiting Room: Open the Participants panel. Admit the instructor individually first, then Admit All for participants when ready.
2 Co-Host: Hover over the instructor's name → three dots (…) → Make Co-Host. Do this before opening the room to participants.
3 Participant Permissions: Arrow next to Participants icon → Host Tools for Participants. Allow: Chat, Rename, Unmute. Disable: Start Video, all recording and transcription options.
4 Screen Share: Click Share Screen → select window → set to Content Only → check Share Sound for videos → Stop Share when done.
5 Polls: More (⋯) → Polls/Quizzes → Launch → End Poll → Share Results → Stop Sharing.
6 Breakout Rooms: More (⋯) → Breakout Rooms → set number of rooms (4–6 participants each) → Assign Automatically → exclude Co-Host → Create → post activity in chat → Open All Rooms.
7 Recording: Click Record → Record to the Cloud. Pause during breaks. Stop when session ends.
8 Mute a Participant: Hover over name in Participants panel → click Mute.
9 Closed Captions: More (⋯) → Show Captions.

Detailed Process Guide

2.1 — Managing the Waiting Room & Admitting Participants

All CLEAR Learning live sessions use the waiting room. Click the Participants icon at the bottom of the screen to open the participant panel.

  • A Waiting Room section lists anyone who has joined but not yet been admitted.

  • To admit everyone at once, click Admit All.

  • To admit a specific person, hover over their name and click Admit.

Admitting the Instructor First: Log in 15 minutes early. When the instructor joins the waiting room, admit only them so you can make them Co-Host and complete a tech check before opening the room to participants.

2.2 — Making the Instructor a Co-Host

Instructors must be Co-Hosts so they can share their screen and manage slides.

  • In the Participants panel, hover over the instructor's name.

  • Click the three dots (…) that appear on the far right of their row.

  • Select Make Co-Host.

Why This Matters: If you lose your internet connection, a Co-Host keeps the session live. Without one, the session ends the moment you are disconnected.

2.4 — Muting a Disruptive Participant

In the Participants panel, hover over the participant's name and click the Mute button that appears.

2.5 — Screen Sharing

  • Click Share Screen in the meeting toolbar.

  • Select the window or screen to share.

  • Set the share option to Content Only (not Desktop).

  • If sharing a video, check Share Sound in the share options on the right before clicking Share.

  • Once sharing, the main Zoom window minimizes to a toolbar. Click the red Stop Share button when done.

2.6 — Closed Captions

Go to More (⋯) in the meeting toolbar and select Show Captions to enable live automatic captions. Use if a participant is hearing impaired.

2.7 — Running Polls & Quizzes

  • Click More (⋯) in the toolbar and select Polls/Quizzes.

  • Hover over the poll you want to run and click Launch.

  • A timer and participation counter display during the poll. Aim for approximately 90% participation before ending a single-question poll — typically 30–45 seconds.

  • Click End Poll, then Share Results to display answers to all participants.

  • Click Stop Sharing when done.

  • To run another poll: click the back arrow (←) in the top left of the polls window, select the next poll, and repeat.

Multi-Question Polls: For polls with multiple questions (e.g., the Report Writing session), remind participants to answer all questions before submitting. The participation display shows total submissions, not per-question responses.

2.8 — Breakout Rooms

  • Click More (⋯) and select Breakout Rooms.

  • Set the number of rooms using the toggle. Aim for 4–6 participants per room (minimum 3 depending on class size).

  • Select Assign Automatically — do not assign manually or let participants choose.

  • Check the option to not include the Co-Host so the instructor stays in the main room with you until they are ready to visit rooms.

  • Click Create.

  • Post the breakout activity prompt in the chat before opening rooms so participants have it when they arrive.

  • Click Open All Rooms when ready.

While Rooms Are Open:

  • Broadcast a message — use the Broadcast button to send a text message to all rooms (e.g., a time warning).

  • Move a participant — hover over their name and click the Move To icon to move them to a different room.

  • Move the instructor into a specific room the same way, when they are ready to visit.

  • Add a room if you need to separate participants further.

  • Plan the next session — use the Plan Next Session button to adjust room count or settings for a follow-up breakout.

Closing Rooms: Clicking Close All Rooms sends a 60-second countdown notice into all rooms. Participants are automatically returned to the main room when the timer expires.

2.9 — Recording a Session

Only record sessions for programs that require a makeup recording (ACRG, ELPR, BCT, SAB). Always record to the cloud, not your local computer.

Step Action
1 Click the Record button in the meeting toolbar.
2 Select Record to the Cloud. A recording indicator will appear in the top left corner confirming recording has started.
3 To pause recording during a break, click the pause icon next to the recording indicator.
4 To stop recording, click the stop icon. You can stop and restart to create separate clips if needed.
5 When the session ends, Zoom will process the recording and email you when it is ready. Download it from the CLEAR Learning Zoom account.
6 Follow the Update & Release Live Session Recordings process guide to upload to Vimeo, edit, and release in TalentLMS.

Note: Recordings of some programs are shared with the current cohort only and are removed after the cohort completes. All other programs use pre-existing recordings — do not record those sessions.
